The Apache's loss functions as the war's casus belli of convenience. CENTCOM's response was calibrated and signaling-oriented: "self-defense strikes" against air defense, radar, and ground-control nodes, not regime or population targets — a proportional, face-saving riposte designed to restore deterrence without committing to open-ended war.
