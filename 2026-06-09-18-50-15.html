Analytical Context

Counter-base warfare is the doctrine of degrading an adversary by saturating its forward air bases rather than seeking decisive battle — precisely the asymmetric logic Iran can sustain with cheap missiles (the Khorramshahr, a liquid-fuel MRBM) against fixed, expensive targets like Israel's Ramat David. Policy Tensor borrows Robert Pape's coercion framework: bombing rarely breaks will directly, but a campaign that drains Gulf oil inventories converts military pressure into economic leverage.

The recurring image is the "noose" — a structural inversion in which the encircled party gains bargaining power as the encircler's costs mount. The closing rhetorical question ("if they do not know it, does it matter?") flags the gap between objective structure and the actors' perceptions, the central problem of deterrence theory.

"Risk appetite of broker-dealers" points to the dealer-balance-sheet school of macro-finance (associated with Hyun Song Shin and the BIS), where intermediaries' willingness to expand leverage — "juicing the machine" — is the real driver of financial conditions. The falsifiable wager (the Q1 2026 print exceeding Q4 2025) is what separates this from commentary: it is a dated, checkable forecast.

Analytical Context

"Elite overproduction" is Peter Turchin's term (structural-demographic theory): when a society mints more credentialed aspirants than there are elite slots, the surplus turns to intra-elite conflict and instability — reframing campus crackdowns not as strength but as a symptom of decay. The "empire on suicide watch" thesis, from political economist Herman Mark Schwartz, locates American power in institutional foundations (the dollar, alliances, rule-of-law credibility) that the leadership is itself eroding.

The rejection of "great man" / "5D chess" readings is methodological: by stressing continuity between an "outlaw" president and the establishment he supposedly defies, Policy Tensor argues outcomes are driven by structure, not personality — the same anti-exceptionalist move that runs through the whole timeline.
