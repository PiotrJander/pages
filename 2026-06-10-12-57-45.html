Analytical Context

The throughline is a counter-base theory of war: precision ballistic missiles in sufficient salvos have made fixed, concentrated airpower — the apron full of "eagles," the regional mega-base — a liability rather than an asset. The recurring "interceptor math" is the crux: ballistic-missile defense (BMD) is finite and expensive per shot, so a large enough salvo always saturates it. That arithmetic drives every downstream claim — abandon or harden Gulf-littoral bases, conserve MRBMs for Israel, expect a calibrated "tit for tat."

Note the analytic posture: he treats Iran as a rational escalation-manager operating a published "150% doctrine" (respond with half-again the inflicted damage), and reads U.S. behavior — water-tank strikes, drone overflights — as evidence of targeting failure rather than strength. The "separate peace" wager (a Trump–Bibi split leaving Israel to fight alone) recurs as the optimistic branch of his decision tree, conditioned entirely on Washington behaving instrumentally rather than ideologically.
